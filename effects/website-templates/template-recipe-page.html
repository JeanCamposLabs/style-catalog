Slow-roast tomato pasta

Serves 415 min prep90 min cook

Ingredients

  • Cherry tomatoes800 g
  • Garlic cloves6
  • Olive oil60 ml
  • Rigatoni400 g

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 140°C.
  2. Toss the tomatoes and garlic in oil, roast for 90 minutes until collapsed.
  3. Cook the pasta until just short of al dente, reserving a cup of water.
  4. Fold everything together, loosening with the pasta water.
